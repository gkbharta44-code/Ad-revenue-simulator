Illustrative simulation only — figures are synthetic, generated live in-browser. This model keeps the source plan's own 60 minutes/day watch-time assumption (10,800 ten-second ads/month/user), but replaces its CPC-priced-as-CPM rate (which implied $36–$64 effective CPM) with a blended real-world rewarded-video CPM of $3–$8. Onboarding follows a 4-year logistic curve to a 6B-user ceiling, far faster than any real consumer app has ever grown (WhatsApp and TikTok each took 5–8 years to reach 1B) — kept this aggressive because that is the horizon requested, not because it is realistic. Nothing here reflects real transaction data or a committed forecast.
